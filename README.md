# Festival Retail Intelligence Dashboard

## Project Overview

Festival Retail Intelligence is a Power BI dashboard project designed to analyze the impact of Indian festivals on retail sales, customer demand, inventory planning, and profitability.

Indian festivals such as Diwali, Navratri, Eid, Christmas, Ganesh Utsav, and Holi can influence customer purchasing behavior and create seasonal changes in retail demand. This project aims to turn retail data into meaningful business insights that can support better planning and decision-making.

## Business Problem

Retail businesses often face challenges during festive periods, including:

- Sudden increases in customer demand.
- Inventory shortages during peak sales periods.
- Excess inventory after festivals.
- Reduced profit margins due to heavy discounts.
- Difficulty planning stock and promotions effectively.

This dashboard helps analyze these business challenges using interactive data visualizations.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze retail sales performance during festival periods.
- Understand changes in customer demand and purchasing patterns.
- Compare sales and profitability across relevant categories and periods.
- Identify potential inventory planning and discount-related issues.
- Support data-driven decisions for retail managers and merchandising teams.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Power BI Desktop** — Dashboard development and visualization.
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX** — Measures and calculations, where applicable.
- **Data Analytics** — Trend analysis and business insight generation.

## Key Business Questions

This project is designed to explore questions such as:

1. How does retail performance change during festival periods?
2. Which festivals or periods show stronger sales performance?
3. How does customer demand vary across relevant retail categories?
4. What patterns can be observed in discounts and profitability?
5. How can retailers improve inventory and merchandising decisions?

## Business Value

The analysis can help retail decision-makers:

- Plan inventory for expected demand changes.
